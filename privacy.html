Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how ShortsClipper ("the app") handles information. ShortsClipper is a personal desktop application used by the owner of the GTAVI YouTube channel to upload short video clips to that channel.

YouTube API Services

ShortsClipper uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos that the channel owner has reviewed and approved, and to set their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.
  • youtube.readonly — to read the signed-in channel's name, to confirm which channel uploads go to.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, messages, subscriptions, contacts, email or any other Google data.

How information is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens and app data are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app's developer.
  • Google user data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Public livestream audio transcripts used to choose highlight moments contain no Google user data.

ShortsClipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes access. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through Google security settings. After revocation, stored tokens no longer work, and they are deleted within 30 days.

Cookies

The app and this website do not use cookies or tracking.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
